> The White Hat Code

1. Permission First

Never test a system you don't own or have written authorization to test.

2. Do No Harm

Don't damage, delete, or leak data. Document and report findings.

3. Stay Legal

Laws like CFAA (US) and GDPR (EU) apply. Know them before you act.

4. Keep Learning

The best hackers are the most curious. Read, lab, repeat.
