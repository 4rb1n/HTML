// 04 · Code of Conduct

The White Hat Code

Skill without ethics is a weapon. These four rules separate hackers from criminals.

01

Permission First

Only test what you own or have written permission for.

02

Do No Harm

Never damage, delete, or leak data. Document and report.

03

Stay Legal

CFAA, CMA, GDPR apply to you. Know them before you act.

04

Keep Learning

The best hackers are the most curious. Read, lab, repeat.
